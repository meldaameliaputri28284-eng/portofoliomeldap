PODCAST GUEST — UWN MEDIA

3. Podcast at UWN Media

An opportunity to participate in a podcast at UWN Media alongside Mrs. Komala Sari, S.M., M.Si., one of the inspiring figures I admire at Universitas Widya Nusantara.

During the podcast, we shared stories and personal experiences about tourism, as well as discussed various topics related to business. It was a valuable opportunity to share experiences, gain new knowledge, and create a memorable moment.

“A memorable experience at UWN Media, where we shared stories and personal experiences about tourism while discussing various topics related to business. I had the opportunity to share my stories with Mrs. Komala Sari, S.M., M.Si., one of the inspiring figures I admire at Universitas Widya Nusantara. It was a meaningful and unforgettable part of my university journey.”
